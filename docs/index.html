SkillDrasil

A skill tree for Valheim. Your deeds feed the roots. Your choices shape the branches.

Get it on Thunderstore Download zip Explore the tree

Valheim 1.0 · BepInEx + Jotunn · English & Русский · Safe to add to and remove from existing saves

Vanilla skills

  • Repeat an action and that skill levels up.
  • Die and you lose levels in everything.
  • No choices: every Viking ends up the same.

SkillDrasil

  • Actions feed five branches; full branches give points.
  • You spend the points on masteries and perks.
  • Death costs progress, never your choices.

The World Tree

Five branches. Masteries are the roots, perks are the crown, and the trunk grows with every point you spend in the branch.

How it works

Earn

Every swing, shot, swim and stew feeds XP into that skill's branch. A full bar grants a branch point, spent only in that branch.

Masteries

Every vanilla skill has 5 ranks worth 20 levels each, so all of Valheim's own skill effects still apply. Gear and mead bonuses stack on top.

Perks

Three per branch, unlocked at 3, 6 and 9 points spent: Berserkgang, Silent Death, Eitr Well, Featherfall and more.

Free points

Every boss felled in your world and every new biome you discover grants a free point for any branch.

Death

You lose part of each branch's unspent XP, never your points. You rise with Severed Roots for 10 minutes: masteries one rank lower, tier 3 perks silent.

Respec

Craft a Rune of Forgetting at the workbench (10 Stone, 10 Resin, 5 Greydwarf Eye) and consume it to refund every point.

A long saga, not an evening

Point n in a branch costs 80 × n1.5 XP. The first points come in minutes; filling a branch takes a whole playthrough.

Safe for your saves

  • Your vanilla skill levels are never written. The tree lives in its own entry in the character file.
  • First load of an existing character: every 20 vanilla levels in a skill become 1 point in that skill's branch.
  • Uninstall and your vanilla skills are exactly as they were. Reinstall and your tree is back.
  • Only Runes of Forgetting in your bags disappear without the mod.

Multiplayer

Every player and the server must run SkillDrasil, same major.minor version. Jotunn refuses mismatched connections, so nobody joins with an unfair advantage. Balance settings come from the server; each character keeps its own tree.

Install

  1. Easiest: install SkillDrasil from Thunderstore with r2modman or the Thunderstore Mod Manager. BepInEx and Jotunn come with it; skip to step 4. Manual install:
  2. Install BepInExPack Valheim.
  3. Install Jotunn.
  4. Back up your characters: …\LocalLow\IronGate\Valheim\characters_local or, with Steam Cloud, Steam\userdata\<id>\892970\remote\characters.
  5. Download the latest release and put SkillDrasil.dll in BepInEx/plugins/SkillDrasil/.
  6. Start the game and press K, or the Skills button in your inventory.
